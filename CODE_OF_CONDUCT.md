# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss technical decisions without attacking people. Assume good intent, welcome different experience levels, and give maintainers enough evidence to reproduce problems.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, deliberate disruption, and repeated bad-faith promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its community. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed without public disclosure unless required for resolution.
